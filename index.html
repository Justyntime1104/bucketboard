★★★ = Starter, ★★ = Sub, ★ = Alternate. Position box: 1=PG, 2=SG, 3=SF, 4=PF, 5=C — subs are matched by position. "Max subs on court" caps how many ★★/★ players can be on the floor at once (swapping all 5 at a break is fine). Target minutes set what each role should play; Auto-Generate scales to fit the game. Tap cells to hand-pick — Auto-Generate keeps your picks and fills the blanks (use Clear Plan for a blank grid). Pressing START at the top of a half loads the planned five automatically. During the game you'll get a reminder 1 minute out and a one-tap confirm 30 seconds before each swap — and if you sub off-script (injury, breather), the rest of the plan rebalances itself around actual minutes played so everyone still gets their time.

ON THE BENCH

Roster and minutes come from your live game — subs made here update the box score too. Tap a photo circle to add or reframe a picture (saved on this device).
Before tip-off, tap players to set your starting five, then press Set Starters to start the clock. During the game, tap to stage subs — bench players go in (green), court players come out (red) — then Commit Subs when the ref waves them in. Each on-court card shows total minutes and the current shift.

Drag tokens to position players — Offense and Defense keep separate spots. Save Formation makes the current layout the default for Load On-Court Players. Record: press ●, move one player through the play in real time, press stop and save. Then press ⟳ Overdub to arm it — the replay and recording both start the instant you grab a token, so your new layer is synced automatically. Drop the ball on a player and it rides with them until you grab it again. Layer a pass per player until everyone moves together. While recording, tap Pick / Cut / Shoot / Pass to stamp an action bubble on the last player you touched. Double-tap a token to remove it.
